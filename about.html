About Us

NST Nails - is a small manicure company founded in Sweden in 2024 and dedicated to providing high-quality, affordable, and personalized nail care services. We strive to make every nail look and feel as good as new, and we believe that every individual deserves a beautiful nail. Our commitment to providing excellent service and customer satisfaction is our top priority. We are always here to help you find the best nail care options for your needs.

Policy Info

Payment


We reserve the right to change prices and services at anytime without notification. We accept Cash or Swish. We don't accept card or personal checks.

Returns


All sale items are final and non-returnable. If you are not happy with your nails, please make us aware of it before you pay. We will adjust them to your satisfaction or remove any enhancements or coatings that have been applied.

Privacy Policy


We will treat your personal information with care and confidentiality. We will not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties without your consent. We will only use your personal information to provide our services, respond to your inquiries, and follow up with you. We will retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill your needs and protect against fraud.

Safety & Hygiene


Please wash your hands thoroughly with water & soap in our sink or take some hand sanitiser thats available at all work stations. All Nailteches wears face mask . All work stations, LED lamps and equipments are disinfected between every customers. Some products are used and discarded after each customer for hygienic reasons.
